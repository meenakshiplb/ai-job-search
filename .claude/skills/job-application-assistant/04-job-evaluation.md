# Job Evaluation Framework

## Scoring Dimensions

Evaluate each job posting against these five dimensions:

### 1. Technical Skills Match (0-100)

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 80-100 | Core requirements are primary skills |
| 60-79 | Most requirements match, 1-2 gaps that are learnable |
| 40-59 | Partial match, significant upskilling needed |
| 0-39 | Fundamental mismatch |

**Strong match areas:** Java, REST APIs, Agile/Scrum delivery, sprint facilitation
**Moderate match areas:** [Add specific frameworks/tools via /setup], CI/CD tooling, cloud basics
**Weak match areas (honest gaps):** CS fundamentals (algorithms, data structures, system design theory), ML/AI production experience, formal architecture credentials

### 2. Experience Match (0-100)

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 80-100 | Direct experience in the same domain and role type |
| 60-79 | Related experience, transferable skills clear |
| 40-59 | Adjacent experience, would need to make the case |
| 0-39 | Unrelated experience |

**Strong:** Senior software engineering in Java/REST, Agile delivery, operational on-call work
**Moderate:** Technical team lead, cross-functional collaboration, API-driven integrations
**Entry-level / needs framing:** AI/ML engineering, product ownership, architecture roles

### 3. Behavioral/Culture Fit (0-100)

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 80-100 | Culture strongly matches behavioral preferences |
| 60-79 | Mixed signals but mostly compatible |
| 40-59 | Some friction areas |
| 0-39 | Significant culture mismatch |

**Red flags to research:** Credential-heavy cultures that devalue practitioner experience, heavy-bureaucracy environments with no room for ownership, roles with no growth path.

### 4. Location & Logistics (Pass/Fail + Notes)
- Vienna-based or remote within Europe: **PASS**
- Hybrid with occasional Vienna office: **PASS**
- Full relocation required: **FAIL** (deal-breaker)
- Frequent international travel: **FLAG** (discuss)
- German language required (B2+): **FLAG** — assess honestly based on current level

### 5. Career Alignment & Motivation (0-100)

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 80-100 | Strongly aligned with career direction, clear growth path |
| 60-79 | Good role but only partially aligned with long-term goals |
| 40-59 | Decent job but doesn't build toward goals |
| 0-39 | Dead end or backwards step |

**Career goals (priority order):**
1. Immediate: Senior Software Engineer (Java/REST) — Vienna or remote Europe
2. Strategic: Transition to AI Engineer over 12-24 months
3. Exploratory: Product Owner or AI-adjacent role if fit is strong

**Tasks that energize:**
- Building real systems (backend, APIs, services)
- Facilitating teams and improving delivery processes
- Learning new technologies and applying them
- Cross-functional collaboration

**Tasks that drain:**
- Pure maintenance with no learning curve
- Heavily siloed work with no team interaction
- Roles with no growth trajectory

**Life situation:** Actively seeking due to layoff — security matters now, but don't take a role that's a career step backwards.

## Weighting
- Technical Skills: 30%
- Experience Match: 25%
- Behavioral Fit: 15%
- Career Alignment: 30%

(Location is pass/fail, not weighted)

## Thresholds
- **Strong Fit** (75+): Definitely apply, tailor everything
- **Good Fit** (60-74): Apply, address gaps in cover letter
- **Moderate Fit** (45-59): Consider carefully, discuss
- **Weak Fit** (30-44): Probably skip unless strategic reasons
- **Poor Fit** (<30): Skip

## Vienna/Austria Market Notes
- **kununu.com** is the dominant employer review platform in the DACH region (more relevant than Glassdoor for Austrian companies)
- Many Vienna tech companies operate in English but list jobs in German — English applications are widely accepted in international tech companies
- Watch for salary: Austria has mandatory salary disclosure in job ads (Kollektivvertrag / KV minimum). The actual offered salary is almost always negotiable above the KV minimum.
- B2B/SaaS companies and US tech firms with Vienna offices often have strong English-first cultures

## Pre-Application: Call the Employer
Consider calling the contact person listed in the posting if:
- Requirements are unclear or ambiguous
- Role description is vague about day-to-day tasks
- There's a named contact who invites questions

Good questions to ask:
- "What are the primary challenges in this role?"
- "Which competencies are most critical for success in this position?"
- "What does success look like in the first 6 months?"
- "How is the team structured and how is work divided?"
