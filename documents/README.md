# Documents Folder

Drop your source materials here before running `/setup`. Claude will read them and build your profile automatically (Path A).

## Subfolders

| Folder | What to put here |
|--------|-----------------|
| `cv/` | Your CV or resume (PDF, DOCX, or .txt) |
| `linkedin/` | LinkedIn profile export (Settings > Data Privacy > Get a copy of your data) |
| `applications/` | Past application materials (for pattern learning) |

## How to export LinkedIn data
1. LinkedIn > Settings & Privacy > Data Privacy > Get a copy of your data
2. Select "Profile" and request archive
3. Extract the ZIP and copy the relevant CSV files into `documents/linkedin/`
